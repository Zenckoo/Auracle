🔮 The Service

In short: a free social companion for Discord, still growing.

Auracle is a free, non-commercial social companion for Discord. It offers a personality trial based on the Big Five model and public scientific items, profile cards you can customize (with a banner, badges and blessings, kind words left by other travelers), comparisons, guild statistics and optional guild roles.

Auracle is currently in public beta: features may change, be added or removed, and bugs may happen. Auracle is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Discord Inc. or Anthropic.

Staying free. Auracle is free today, and the goal is to keep it free for as long as possible. If Auracle grows and its running costs become too high, we reserve the right to open a page for voluntary donations, and to offer some new or existing features as optional paid features. In that case:

  • 🔮 the trial, your results and your right to see, export and erase your data will always stay free;
  • 📢 any paid feature will be announced in advance in 📜 Updates, with its price;
  • 🙅 you will never be charged without your clear agreement;
  • 📜 paid features will come with their own terms, and the Privacy Policy will be updated first.

🚫 Forbidden uses

In short: Auracle is for fun and connection, never for judging, dating or abuse.

You must never use Auracle or its results to:

  • 🏢 hire, select, rank, grade, exclude or reward anyone, for example in a job, a school, a team, a server role or a moderation decision;
  • 🩺 diagnose, treat or assess anyone's health, mental health or abilities;
  • 💔 find a date, or for anything romantic or sexual. Auracle is never a dating app;
  • 🎭 impersonate someone, or take the trial on someone else's behalf without their consent.

You must also never:

  • 🤖 spam the bot, automate it, scrape it, or overload it on purpose;
  • 📬 spam Feedback: Feedback is limited to 5 messages every 24 hours. Trying to send more triggers an automatic 30-day block from Auracle, without warning. Insulting, abusive or meaningless Feedback can also lead to a block;
  • 🐞 exploit bugs or security flaws, or try to access data or systems that are not yours (if you find a flaw, please report it with Feedback instead 💜);
  • 🧩 copy, resell or redistribute Auracle or its content, or build a service that pretends to be Auracle.

Content you add to your card must never include: NSFW or sexual content, shocking or violent content, hate, harassment or insults, someone else's private information, spam or advertising, or anything illegal.

Banners follow the same rules. In addition, you must only upload an image you have the right to use, and never a photo of another person without their consent, or anything that shows private information (such as a face, a school, an address or a license plate that is not yours to share). Uploads are limited to 5 every 24 hours.

Blessings are for kind words only. They must never be used to flirt, ask for a date, ask for private information or contact details, mock, pressure or harass anyone, or advertise. Blessings are limited to 5 every 24 hours.
