🔮 What Auracle is

Auracle is a free social companion for Discord, made by Zenckoo. It starts with a personality trial based on the Big Five, then lets you share a card with your community.

  • A fun estimate, never a diagnosis. Auracle is not a psychological, medical or professional assessment.
  • Never a tool to judge people. Results must not be used to hire, select, rank or exclude anyone.
  • Discord's age rules apply: you must be old enough to have a Discord account in your country.

🤝 Respect

  • No mocking someone for their class, their traits or their card.
  • No discrimination, ever: not for gender, who someone loves, skin, faith, body, age, origins, or anything else.
  • Never use someone's results against them.
  • The rules of each server, Discord's Terms of Service and Discord's Community Guidelines apply to Auracle too.

Part 3 / 7

🚫 Not allowed on cards

Card customization is coming soon. Whatever you add to your card (banner, phrase, vibe, kind words left on other cards...) must never include:

  • NSFW or sexual content;
  • shocking or violent content;
  • hate, harassment or insults;
  • someone else's private information;
  • spam or advertising.

Auracle is never a dating app: using it to find a date or for anything sexual is not allowed.

Part 4 / 7

🛡️ Reports

With card customization, every card will have a Report button. Reports go straight to Zenckoo, who reviews each one personally. Until then, you can report a problem with 📬 Feedback in /auracle.

If these rules are broken, the content can be removed, customization can be blocked, and for serious or repeated cases, access to Auracle can be removed.

For anything unsafe on Discord itself, you can also block the person, tell your server's moderators, and report it to Discord.

🛠️ No guarantee

Auracle is a free project, made with care, and still a work in progress. It is offered as it is:

  • bugs can happen, and features can change, pause or end;
  • Auracle can be offline sometimes;
  • what you choose to make public can be seen by others, so share only what you are comfortable with.
