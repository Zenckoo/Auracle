👤 Who runs Auracle

In short: Auracle is made by Zenckoo, who is responsible for your data.

Auracle (the "Service") is created, operated and maintained by an independent developer known as Zenckoo ("we", "us"). Zenckoo acts as the data controller within the meaning of Article 4(7) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and is responsible for deciding how and why your personal data is processed.

To protect the developer's own privacy, Zenckoo's full legal identity is not published online. It will be disclosed on request to any competent data protection authority, and to any user exercising their rights under the GDPR when it is needed for that request.

Auracle is a free, non-commercial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Discord Inc. or Anthropic.

📦 What Auracle collects

In short: only what the bot needs to work, listed here in full.

When you use the bot:

  • 🆔 Your Discord user ID: a number that identifies your account. Your username, nickname and avatar are read from Discord when a card is displayed, and are not stored.
  • 📝 Your trial answers: one value from 1 to 5 per statement, the trial version (Standard or Grand), your progress, and the dates you started and last answered.
  • 🔮 Your results: your trait and facet scores, percentiles, four-letter type, class, and the date you finished.
  • ⚙️ Your settings: whether your card is public or private, whether your hidden secret is shown, and whether you wear your guild role.
  • 🏰 The servers where you used Auracle: the server ID and the last time you used Auracle there.

When you customize your card (🎨 Customize in /auracle), only what you choose to fill in, all optional:

  • ✨ Appearance: your fantasy title and accent color.
  • 💬 About me: your short sentence and your current vibe.
  • 🎮 Tastes & fun: favorite games, anime or series, music and people, "Ask me about", what kind of company you are looking for, and your DM preference.
  • 🕰️ Practical: a broad time zone (never your city), the languages you speak, and your birthday (day and month only, never the year).
  • 🌈 Identity and origins: only with your explicit consent, see Identity and origins.

For servers whose staff turn on guild roles: the server ID, whether guild roles are on, and the IDs of the four roles Auracle created.

When you send Feedback: your message, your Discord user ID and username. They are posted in a private channel on Auracle's own Discord server, so they are stored by Discord. Auracle also records the time of each message for 24 hours, only to apply the limit of 5 messages every 24 hours.

When you report a card: the reason you chose, the details you wrote (if any), your Discord user ID and username, the reported traveler's user ID and username, their class, what their card showed at the time of the report, and the server ID. They are posted in a private moderation channel on Auracle's own Discord server, so they are stored by Discord. Auracle also records the time of each report and whose card it was about for 24 hours, only to apply the limits (5 reports every 24 hours, one report per card).

If you are blocked (see the Terms of Service): your Discord user ID, the reason, and the start and end dates of the block.

On the website: no account, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. The site and the bot are hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical connection data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks. Auracle does not use this data to identify or follow you.

🚫 What Auracle never collects

In short: Auracle never reads your conversations and knows nothing about you outside the trial.

Auracle never collects:

  • the content of your messages, channels, DMs or voice chats (Auracle does not request access to message content);
  • your email address, phone number, real name, address or payment information;
  • your Discord friends list, connections or activity;
  • your precise location;
  • any special category of data under Article 9 GDPR (such as health, religion, sexual orientation or ethnic origin) without your explicit consent. The trial never asks about any of these. The only exception is Identity and origins, which you may choose to fill in yourself (see the next part);
  • your birth year, your age, your city or your address.

Auracle uses no cookies, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells, rents or trades your data.

🌈 Identity and origins

In short: entirely optional, private by default, only with your explicit consent, and erased the moment you change your mind.

In 🎨 Customize, you may choose to share who you are on your card. This part is entirely optional, and nothing is saved unless you fill it in yourself.

What it can contain:

  • 🏷️ Pronouns (for example she/her, he/him, they/them, neopronouns, or your own words).
  • 🌈 Gender identity (for example woman, man, non-binary, genderfluid, agender, questioning, or your own words).
  • 💞 Sexual and romantic orientation (for example gay, lesbian, bisexual, pansexual, asexual, aromantic, questioning, or your own words).
  • 🌍 Origins: one or more countries or cultural heritages, shown with their flags. Never a city or an address.

Why this needs special care. Sexual orientation and ethnic origin are special categories of personal data under Article 9 GDPR. Auracle treats pronouns and gender identity with exactly the same care.

Explicit consent. The legal basis is your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR). The first time you open this page, a screen explains everything written here, and nothing can be saved until you tap I agree. Auracle records the date you agreed, only to prove your consent.

How it is protected:

  • 🔒 Private by default, with its own visibility switch, separate from your card. Even if your card is public, this part stays private until you decide otherwise.
  • ⚠️ Before it becomes public, Auracle shows a reminder that it will be visible to everyone in the servers where your card can be seen.
  • 🚫 It is never used in Compare, Guilds, guild roles or statistics, never used to sort, match, recommend or judge anyone, and never linked to your trial results.
  • 🔗 Links are not allowed in the "own words" fields. Hateful or mocking content breaks the Terms of Service and can be reported.
  • 🛠️ Like all data, it is never sent to any AI and never used to train any AI.

Withdrawing your consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time with the Withdraw consent button on the same page: all your identity and origins data is then erased immediately. /forget erases it too. Withdrawing does not affect what was shown before.

Younger travelers. In some countries, young people need a parent's or guardian's agreement to give this consent (in France, under 15). If that is your case, please ask them first, or simply leave this part empty.

Part 7 / 16

🧭 Profiling

In short: your class is a fun estimate, and it never decides anything about you.

Estimating personality traits from your answers is considered profiling under Article 4(4) GDPR. Here is how Auracle handles it:

  • 🔢 Your results come from a fixed, public formula applied the same way to everyone, based on published research (see the Codex).
  • 🚫 Auracle makes no decision based solely on automated processing that has a legal effect on you or affects you in a similarly significant way (Article 22 GDPR). Your class is for fun and self-reflection only.
  • 🔮 It is not a diagnosis and not a psychological, medical or professional assessment.
  • 🗝️ You can retake the trial, keep your results private, object to the processing, or erase everything with /forget at any time.
  • ⚠️ Servers and users must never use Auracle results to select, rank, exclude or judge anyone. This is forbidden by the Terms of Service.

The only automatic measure Auracle applies is the Feedback spam block. It depends only on the number of messages sent, never on your answers or results, and you can ask for a human review by email.

🤖 AI transparency

In short: no AI ever reads your answers or decides anything about you.

  • 🚫 No AI processes your data. Your answers and results are never sent to any AI model or AI company. Your results come from a fixed mathematical formula, not from an AI.
  • 🧠 No training. Your data is never used to train, test or improve any AI model.
  • 🛠️ How AI is used. Auracle's code and texts are written with the help of Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic. Zenckoo reviews and approves every change before it goes live.
  • 🔐 A strict line. Claude works only on Auracle's code, texts and database structure. It never has access to users' personal data. All testing is done with the developer's own account.
  • 🎨 No AI art. Auracle's art and illustrations are always made by humans, never by AI.
  • 📖 More about this choice in the Codex page Auracle and AI.

👀 Who can see what

In short: private by default, and you decide what others see.

  • 🔒 Private card (default): only you can see it.
  • 🌍 Public card: anyone in a server with Auracle can view it with /profile or the menu.
  • 🗝️ Hidden secret: shown on your public card only if you choose to.
  • 🎨 What you add in Customize: follows your card. Shown only if your card is public, otherwise only you can see it.
  • 🌈 Identity and origins: private by default, with their own switch. Shown only if your card is public and you chose to make them public.
  • 🎂 Your birthday: day and month only. On that day, your card says so. Auracle never sends a message about it.
  • 🔍 Your answers and your 30 facets: never shown to anyone else.
  • 🤝 Compare and 🏰 Guilds: posted in the channel, visible to its members. They only use public cards. Private cards are never shown or counted.
  • 🏰 Guild roles: visible to the server's members, like any Discord role.
  • 📬 Feedback: read only by Zenckoo.
  • 🚩 Reports: read only by Zenckoo. The reported traveler is never told who reported them.
  • 🛠️ Zenckoo, as the developer, has technical access to the database, used only to run, fix and protect the Service.

🛡️ Security

In short: your data is protected with care.

  • 🔐 All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • ✍️ Every request from Discord is verified with a cryptographic signature, so no one can pretend to be Discord.
  • 🗝️ The bot's secret keys are stored encrypted by Cloudflare, and never in the code.
  • 🎯 Auracle asks for as few Discord permissions as possible: only Manage Roles, used only for its four guild roles.
  • 👤 Only Zenckoo has access to the database.
  • 🚨 If a data breach ever puts your rights at risk, we will notify the competent authority within 72 hours and inform affected users without undue delay (Articles 33 and 34 GDPR).

🗝️ Your rights

In short: your data belongs to you.

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • 👁️ Access your data and get a copy of it (Article 15): tap 📦 My data in 🔒 Privacy, and Auracle sends you a private file with everything it keeps
  • ✏️ Correct it (Article 16), for example by retaking the trial
  • 🧹 Erase it (Article 17): use /forget, everything is deleted instantly
  • ⏸️ Restrict its processing (Article 18)
  • 📦 Receive it in a portable format (Article 20): the 📦 My data file is in JSON, a standard format
  • 🙅 Object to its processing (Article 21)
  • ↩️ Withdraw your consent anytime (Article 7), for example by making your card private again, or with Withdraw consent for Identity and origins, which erases them at once

To use these rights, send a message with 📬 Feedback in /auracle, or write to auracle.contact@gmail.com. We will answer within one month (Article 12).

You can also file a complaint with a data protection authority: in France, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or the authority of your country.

🌱 Younger travelers

In short: Auracle is built to be safe for teens, and private by default.

  • 🔞 You must meet Discord's minimum age to use Auracle (13 in most countries, higher in some). Auracle does not knowingly collect data from anyone under that age. If we learn that it happened, the data is deleted.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 In some countries, young people need a parent's or guardian's agreement to share personal data online (in France, under 15). If that is your case, please ask them before making your card public or filling in Identity and origins.
  • 🛡️ Everything is private by default. Auracle never lets users message each other, never shows your answers or facets, and reminds younger travelers to keep their results private and to talk to a trusted adult if anything feels heavy.
  • 💜 Support resources are always available in the Codex, on the page If things feel heavy.
